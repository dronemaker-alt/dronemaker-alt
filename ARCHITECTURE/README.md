## Document Standard

Architecture documents should make these boundaries explicit:

- Project scope and exclusions
- Hardware assumptions
- Subsystems and responsibilities
- Electrical, mechanical, data, and control interfaces
- Known risks and unresolved decisions
- Planned experiments and pass/fail evidence

Implementation records and measured results belong in [`projects/`](../projects/) or [`LAB-NOTES/`](../LAB-NOTES/). Outside designs remain research references until their useful ideas are converted into explicit Drone Libre requirements and experiments.
