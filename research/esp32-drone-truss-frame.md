# ESP32 Truss Frame Concept

## Source
Instagram reel by aadityabharti_01 showing an evolution of an ESP32-based drone frame.

## Observations
- Lightweight open truss construction.
- Large central electronics bay suitable for ESP32/LilyGO hardware.
- Tall landing gear provides clearance for downward sensors.
- Modular arm construction appears optimized for 3D printing.
- Front LED 'eyes' could be repurposed as system status indicators.

## Drone Libre Ideas
- Integrate LilyGO T-Display P4.
- Support H743 or ESP32 flight controller.
- GPS mast and optical flow/ToF sensor mounts.
- Rear sliding battery tray.
- Heat-set brass inserts for durability.

Potential role: Small experimental platform between the Disir micro platform and the Jotun research quad.
