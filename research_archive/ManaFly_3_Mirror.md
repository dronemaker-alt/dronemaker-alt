## Cali-Spider Transfer Experiment

Compare a conventional printed leg-link specimen with a topology-optimized specimen:

- use identical material, layer height, orientation, and target mass;
- record filament mass, print time, dimensions, and post-processing;
- test bending stiffness, joint pullout, impact resistance, and failure mode;
- repeat in PETG, ASA, and Nylon-CF as practical;
- retain photographs and raw measurements in the Cali-Spider prototype test log.

Adopt geometry only when it improves a measured requirement without creating unacceptable print, repair, or inspection difficulty.
